VOLUNTEER ROLES

Reading Buddy requires communication and patience skills,
with at least two available hours per week.

Reading Buddy volunteers support reading activities.

Event Support requires teamwork and organization skills,
with at least four available hours per week.

Event Support volunteers help with programme events.

Digital Help requires computer_basics and communication skills,
with at least three available hours per week.

Digital Help volunteers support basic computer activities.

A role matches only when all required skills and the minimum
weekly availability are satisfied.

These are fictional classroom programme rules.
